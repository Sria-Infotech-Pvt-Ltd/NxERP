## Facts, assumptions and decisions worth carrying forward

- FACT: Frappe, ERPNext and the upstream Helpdesk code were not edited. The Helpdesk fork edits are listed in `docs/adr/ADR-004-amendment-1.md`.
- FACT: a Helpdesk-only user's emailed login link used to land on a no-access page (it bypassed the router); every login that ends in a redirect now goes through `/nxerp/go` (`nxerp.routing.after_request`, tested).
- FACT: Frappe caches site config per worker for 60 s, so the kill-switch takes up to a minute.
- ASSUMPTION: Helpdesk parity items b to e work as their author intended; this was seen for item b only.
- DECISION (owner to ratify): versioning, changelog and flaky-test policies in `docs/guides/release-process.md` and `docs/guides/testing.md`; `AUDIT_ENFORCE` stays off until the advisories are triaged.
